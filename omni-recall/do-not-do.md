- **Do not introduce monetization**: Never add Stripe, paywalls, in-app purchases, paid tiers, or pricing prompts. TheLampStand is 100% free under a strict Mission Lock.
- **Do not add telemetry or tracking**: Never add Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, Meta pixels, or surveillance scripts. Zero PII collection.
- **Do not use em-dashes (—) or en-dashes (–)**: Enforce natural commas, colons, or clean sentences globally across all UI text, metadata, and documentation.
- **Do not invent or composite alternate logo assets**: Use exclusively `/images/wordmark-logo.png` for header branding.
- **Do not bypass safety gates or crisis fail-safes**: Never alter the emergency response protocol for sensitive self-harm or medical queries.
- **Do not create parallel state machines**: All agent surfaces must consume `useAgentController()`. Never manage disconnected `useState` instances in pages.
- **Do not hallucinate Scripture**: All reflections must ground in canonical texts and cite verified chapter and verse references.
- **Do not add unapproved heavy dependencies**: Maintain lean client bundles and local-first performance.
- **Do not alter visual UI/UX during SEO or refactoring tasks**: Keep all layouts, canvases, colors, and components 100% stable unless explicitly requested.
