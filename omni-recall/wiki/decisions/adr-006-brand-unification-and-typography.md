# ADR-006: Brand Unification, Wordmark Standard, and Strict Zero Em-Dash Typography

## Status: Accepted

## Context
Inconsistent brand casing (`LampStand` vs `TheLampStand`), composite flame logo icons, and ubiquitous em-dashes (`—`) weakened visual and editorial polish.

## Decision
1. Standardize brand name to `TheLampStand` globally.
2. Use exclusively `/images/wordmark-logo.png` for header branding.
3. Ban em-dashes (`—`) and en-dashes (`–`) globally across UI copy, data libraries, and documentation, enforcing natural commas, colons, or clean sentences.

## Consequences
Elevates aesthetic polish and humanizes pastoral messaging.
