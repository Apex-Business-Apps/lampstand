# Concept: Gentle Mode & Neurodivergent Alignment

## Core Concept
Spiritual growth is not a game. TheLampStand provides a "Gentle Mode" toggle in Settings designed for users who experience anxiety or distress from gamified app mechanics.

## Design Rules
- Hides streak counters, fire icons, and urgency badges.
- Replaces pressure with still encouragement.
- Preserves full practice logging and presence tracking quietly in local storage without display shaming.
