# Rejected Pattern: Em-Dash & En-Dash Typography in UI Copy

## Why Rejected
Overuse of em-dashes (`—`) creates stilted, robotic, or overly academic phrasing that impairs empathetic readability and causes stylistic inconsistency.

## Alternative
Natural commas, colons, or clean complete sentences. Enforced automatically by `sanitizeAgentText()` in `Grounding.ts`.
