# Architecture Node: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & SEO Architecture

## Overview
TheLampStand implements an enterprise-grade SEO and GEO architecture designed to maximize direct citations and rich snippets in Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, and traditional search engines.

## Key Components

1. **Schema.org Multi-Entity Graph (`index.html`)**:
   - `WebSite`: Canonical URL, alternate aliases, search action.
   - `Organization`: APEX Business Systems Ltd., logo, headquarters (Edmonton, AB), `sameAs` entity backlinks (GitHub repository, Twitter, company page), `knowsAbout` theological/technical taxonomy.
   - `WebApplication` / `SoftwareApplication`: Lifestyle / Religion category, $0 free pricing, OS compatibility.
   - `BreadcrumbList`: 8-level canonical site structure.
   - `HowTo`: Step-by-step contemplative prayer practice guide.
   - `FAQPage`: 8 high-intent answer-first semantic Q&As.

2. **AI Crawler Permissions (`public/robots.txt`)**:
   - Welcomes all major AI engines: Googlebot, Google-Extended, Bingbot, Applebot, Applebot-Extended, GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, PerplexityBot, CCBot, Diffbot, Bytespider, YouBot, cohere-ai.

3. **Canonical XML Sitemap (`public/sitemap.xml`)**:
   - Indexes 16 public canonical URLs with daily/weekly change frequencies and priority weighting.
