## Homiletic Sermon Collection (`SERMON_LIBRARY`)
Every sermon draft provides structured:
- **Title**: Memorable, evocative headline.
- **Reflection**: Deep theological and historical exposition of the passage.
- **Relevance**: Concrete application to modern vocational and relational life.
- **Prayer**: Intimate, direct prayer sealing the reflection.
- **Adaptive Tone Suffix**: Dynamically adapts between `balanced`, `gentle`, and `traditional` modes based on profile preferences.
